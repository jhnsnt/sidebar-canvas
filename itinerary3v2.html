Monday, August 24, 2026
8:15 AM

Board the private motorcoach at the hotel and depart for a full day of sightseeing across the National Mall.

Admission to the Top of George Washington Monument

Ride the elevator 500 feet up the nation's tallest obelisk for a sweeping view of the National Mall, the Potomac River, and the surrounding monuments.

10:30 AM

A self-guided walk through the western half of the National Mall, taking in the Lincoln Memorial steps, the Vietnam Veterans and Korean War Veterans Memorials, and the World War II Memorial.

1:00 PM

Visit the National Museum of African American History and Culture

Explore the Smithsonian's newest museum, tracing African American history from the transatlantic slave trade through the civil rights movement to today, inside its striking bronze corona building.

Stroll the Tidal Basin path past the Jefferson Memorial's marble rotunda, the sculpted rooms of the FDR Memorial, and the granite likeness of Dr. Martin Luther King Jr.

4:30 PM

Check in and settle into your rooms at the Embassy Suites Washington D.C. Georgetown for the next two nights.

6:45 PM

Cheer on the Washington Nationals under the lights at Nationals Park to cap off the first day.
