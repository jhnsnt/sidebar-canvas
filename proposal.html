Airfare is included in your package. Your Landmark Travel Consultant will confirm whether the airfare shown is based on a current flight option or an airfare allocation included for planning purposes.

During the proposal and Open Enrollment period, airfare and flight schedules remain subject to availability and are not yet secured. Once Open Enrollment concludes and your group’s confirmed traveler count and deposits are received, Landmark will secure the appropriate number of seats and provide your group’s confirmed flight details.

Because airline pricing, schedules, and seat availability can change, flight options shown during the planning stage may differ from the final contracted itinerary.

Airfare included in this proposal is based on current market pricing and sample flight routing at the time of quoting and is provided for trip package budgeting purposes. Because airlines only allow group airfare to be held for a limited time and require traveler deposits and information before confirming space, airfare is not secured during the proposal or open enrollment stage.

Once open enrollment closes, Landmark reviews current airline availability and prepares the best available flight options based on your confirmed traveler count. We present our recommended itinerary to the Trip Organizer for approval before securing airfare, ensuring you are included in the decision-making process. Final flight schedules, routes, airline carriers, and pricing remain subject to airline availability until ticketing. While no tour operator can guarantee airfare until tickets are issued, Landmark actively advocates for the best available routing and promptly communicates any airline updates.

Landmark Educational Tours contracts motorcoach transportation through DOT-approved carriers who follow FMCSA driving-hour requirements. Landmark prioritizes reputable, high-quality motorcoach partners; however, carriers are subject to availability and may vary based on your travel dates and destination. Driver gratuity, required driver hotel accommodations, and itinerary-related parking are included in all packages that include motorcoach transportation. If your school or district requires or prefers a specific motorcoach company, please let your Landmark Travel Consultant know as early as possible, as this may affect availability and pricing. Your Travel Consultant can also confirm which carrier was used to prepare your current proposal. A relief driver is automatically included when required by the itinerary and applicable driving-hour regulations.
