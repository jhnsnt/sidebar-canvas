Travel protection

Peace of mind, standard

At Landmark Educational Tours, we believe peace of mind should come standard. That's why all U.S.-based travelers automatically receive post-departure travel protection in their travel package through our partnership with Battleface, at no additional cost. Included coverage helps protect travelers during their journey with benefits such as trip interruption, trip delays, missed connections, emergency medical support, and more.

For families seeking even greater flexibility, optional pre-departure cancellation protection plans are available during registration:

Pre-Departure Cancellation Protection

Provides up to 100% reimbursement if a traveler must cancel prior to departure for an eligible covered reason.

Cancel For Any Reason (CFAR)

Our most flexible option — cancel prior to departure for any reason and receive a 75% refund per the plan's terms and conditions.

With Landmark, your investment is protected both before and during travel, so organizers and families can focus on creating unforgettable memories, not worrying about the unexpected.
